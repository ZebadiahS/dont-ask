Adams Apple

I enjoy making home-brewed hard cider. As a design project, I created a logo and label for my bottled cider. I called the brand Adams Apple as a pun to emphasize how I view brewing cider as a fun and quirky hobby. To emphasize the homemade nature of the cider, I made the label from textured craft paper and created patterns by deconstructing the logo.

I created the cider itself and designed its visual form, originating both the content and its presentation. The bottled cider becomes a place, an object where my personal relationship to cider-making, my sense of enjoyment with the wordplay, and my values around handmade craft are all made visible through the unified creation of product and design.

AI&&ME

Created from a patchwork of materials and surfaces transferred with charcoal, this deconstructed book and its contents stand as an expression of an interest in grounding within the material world. It is a stark contrast to the use of AI within the written content of the book. The extensive analog process of the creation and presentation of the piece stands in strong contrast to the fast and digital nature of AI.
