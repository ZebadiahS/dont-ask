Lists/Glossaries

A poster of glossary terms I created that lists a glossary of terms and slang from mountain biking, skiing, and hiking. As I designed the poster, I layered my own emotional relationship onto this content through visual choices. I used the color green to connect to the green line of the MBTA, linking the urban transit system I use to the outdoor activities I was missing. I also created vertical lines using typographic symbols that reminded me of the tracks each activity leaves on the ground—ski tracks, bike treads, hiking trails—and aligned the terms and slang for each activity along those visual tracks.

The glossary terms already existed, but through design, I began to reveal my own imagined connection between city life and the outdoor recreations these terms and slang come from, allowing people to imagine and experience reading the terms through a new lens.

Chair Pants

Chair Pants are essentially exactly what the name implies. It idea was to combine something of a camp chair with a pair of pants. There are supports on the back of the legs down to the knee. Connected above the waist of the pants is a chair back with a chord system and a clasp that allows the user to connect the top of the chair back to a spot just above the front of the knee, allowing the legs to support the back of the chair.

The design is based on the idea that you should be able to sit comfortably anywhere you go, carrying your chair with you with ease. Someone might use the chair pants to rest during a long walk, while someone else might use them to watch a street performance. They enable the wearer to imagine and experience these existing places in a completely new way.

Lynn Woods

Lynn Woods is a reservation in Lynn, MA, just north of Boston. It has a number of interesting features and landmarks as well as an extensive trail system. But, what if what determined landmarks within the reservation wasn’t existing physical features, and instead the experiences people choose to share? This project combined an ever-changing wayfinding system influenced by a platform that visitors can use to share their Lynn Woods experiences, creating a network of shared space and shared experience that, in turn, influences future visitors.
